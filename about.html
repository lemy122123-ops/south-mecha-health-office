About the Office

The South Mecha Woreda Health Office is responsible for coordinating, supporting and monitoring health services and public health activities within the woreda.

The office works with primary health care units, health centers, health posts, communities and partners to improve access, quality, equity and use of essential health services.

Our role

  • Health service coordination and support
  • Health planning and performance monitoring
  • Health information and data use
  • Disease prevention and control
  • Health promotion and community engagement
  • Supportive supervision and quality improvement
