ABOUT THE OFFICE

Serving the community through a coordinated health system

South Mecha Woreda Health Office coordinates and supports health services, health facilities, community health activities, disease prevention, health promotion, health information and performance improvement across the woreda.

This website is designed to provide the public, partners and health professionals with reliable institutional information and approved health updates.
